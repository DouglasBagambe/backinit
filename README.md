# BackInIt

A multi-sport platform for official viewing, scores and following your sport.

Watch leads with genuine videos from nine official publishers across seven sports, with consent-gated embeds and clear provider restrictions. Provider adapters supply Bundesliga and Formula 1 scores/schedules when available. Accounts include favorites, recovery codes, privacy controls and opt-in in-app updates. The original demo is a separate, explicit edition. **This is not complete global coverage: guaranteed live feeds, most structured sports feeds, licensed syndicated news and direct live sports broadcasts remain unavailable.** Public launch requires further security, rights and infrastructure work and founder approval.
